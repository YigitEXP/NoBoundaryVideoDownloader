🇺🇸 The Story Behind
Every time I tried to download a long video, I hit the same walls: "Videos over 2 hours not allowed," "Pay for HD quality," or those sketchy sites covered in annoying ads. As a computer engineering student, I decided to build my own way out. No Boundary was born to crush those limits and provide a clean, unlimited downloading experience.

🚀 How to Run

Download: Get the standalone .exe right here.

Run: Just double-click main.exe. (Everything is bundled: no Python or FFmpeg installation required—I've got you covered!)

Enjoy: Paste the URL, choose your path, and let it rip.

⚡ Why No Boundary?

No Limits: 2 hours? 10 hours? Doesn't matter. Download it all.

Ad-Free: No clutter, no "Go Pro" pop-ups, just code.

All-in-One: FFmpeg is pre-bundled for a true portable experience.

High Speed: Uses threading to fetch and merge your videos as fast as possible.
